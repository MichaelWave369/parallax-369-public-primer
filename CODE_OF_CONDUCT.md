# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people learning, building, testing, documenting, and respectfully challenging ideas. Participation should leave the work clearer and the people involved better treated.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and decisions without attacking people.
- Preserve uncertainty and dissent rather than forcing false agreement.
- Be patient with newcomers and explain specialized terms.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship, and publication boundaries.
- Correct mistakes openly and proportionately.
- Keep claims within the scope of available evidence.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption.

## Evidence disagreements

Disagreement is allowed and often useful. When challenging a claim:

1. identify the exact statement;
2. distinguish factual, interpretive, and value-based disagreement;
3. provide evidence or reasoning when available;
4. state uncertainty honestly;
5. avoid presenting status or confidence as proof.

## Privacy and protected material

Never pressure contributors to disclose private prompts, internal systems, personal conversations, client information, credentials, or unpublished work. Suspected boundary violations should be reported privately under `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Responses should be proportionate and may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, Pages content, and other spaces where someone is representing or contributing to this project.
