# Public Release Boundary

**Status:** Controlling public-release rule for this repository  
**Applies to:** Every file, commit, issue, pull request, release, website asset, example, and contribution

## Purpose

This repository teaches a limited public expression of the Parallax 3–6–9 Method. It must never become a direct mirror, partial export, or reconstruction path for private Parallax systems.

## The publication rule

> Publish the method, public contracts, blank templates, and synthetic examples. Keep the internal engine, accumulated intelligence, private provenance, and operational machinery private.

## Allowed public material

Material may be published when it was created specifically for this public primer and contains only:

- high-level explanations of Specify, Build, and Prove;
- public governance principles and claim boundaries;
- blank, simplified templates;
- synthetic examples with fictional names and invented data;
- public-safe diagrams and educational website content;
- generic validation checklists;
- independently authored code whose operation does not reveal private systems.

## Prohibited material

Do not publish or reconstruct:

- private skill instructions, system prompts, hidden rubrics, or orchestration logic;
- internal agent or swarm-office behavior;
- private Parallax canon, ledgers, constitutions, validators, schemas, or research;
- private project files, acceptance evidence, decision history, or implementation receipts;
- personal conversations, names, identifying details, client information, or connected-account data;
- secrets, credentials, tokens, endpoints, local paths, deployment configuration, or security details;
- unreleased product designs or capability-enabling operational instructions;
- material copied from a private repository merely because sensitive lines were removed.

## No direct export

Public content should be **independently authored for publication**. A sanitized copy of an internal artifact can still leak structure, terminology, identifiers, assumptions, or capabilities. Therefore:

1. Do not mirror or fork a private Parallax repository into this repository.
2. Do not automate exports from private repositories without an explicit allowlist and human review.
3. Do not paste private prompts into issues, discussions, commit messages, or pull-request descriptions.
4. Do not use real private projects as public examples.

## Mandatory release gate

Before public merge or release, a human reviewer must answer **yes** to all of the following:

- **Purpose:** Was this artifact created for the public primer?
- **Privacy:** Is it free of personal, client, conversation, and project-private information?
- **Capability:** Does it avoid revealing internal orchestration or operational logic?
- **Canon:** Does it avoid unreleased Parallax doctrine, schemas, and accumulated intelligence?
- **Security:** Is it free of secrets, endpoints, paths, and sensitive configuration?
- **Claims:** Are all claims scoped to the evidence actually presented?
- **License:** Is every included work eligible for its stated license?
- **Authority:** Has a human explicitly approved publication?

A single **no**, **unknown**, or unresolved concern blocks publication.

## Certification boundary

Use, adaptation, or completion of this primer does not mean that Parallax has validated, certified, endorsed, approved, or audited a project. Only an explicit, separately documented review can make such a statement.

## Incident response

When prohibited material is discovered:

1. Stop further distribution and do not quote or reproduce the material.
2. Notify the repository owner privately.
3. Remove the material from the current branch or release.
4. Rotate any exposed credentials immediately.
5. Assess whether Git history, caches, forks, releases, or Pages deployments require remediation.
6. Record a public-safe incident receipt without repeating the sensitive content.

## Human authority

No automation, collaborator, model, or workflow may decide by itself that questionable material is safe to publish. Final publication authority remains human.
